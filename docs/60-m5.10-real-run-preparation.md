## K. Stop and cleanup

- **Normal end:** the workflow rests (COMPLETED / WAITING_HUMAN / FAILED). Close the window; the app quits
  without a dialog once it owns no live work.
- **Stop during the run:** press **STOP** in the Workflows detail and confirm with OK; the result is STOPPED, and
  BridgeEngine stops its CLIs. **PAUSE** takes effect at the next iteration boundary. Closing the window
  while it runs shows the quit dialog: **Hủy** keeps it running; **STOP workflow và thoát** stops and quits.
- **Never** `taskkill /T` or kill processes by hand. If something hangs, use STOP, then RESUME to reconcile.
- **Afterwards (operator):**
  1. Review `git -C "<P>" diff` and keep or revert the change yourself.
  2. Keep `.ai-bridge/` (gitignored) until the evidence has been written into docs/59.
  3. For D1, remove the worktree only after that: `git -C "D:/000_AI Agent/bdsdanang" worktree remove "D:/000_AI Agent/bdsdanang-m510"`.
- Then, and only then, continue with the remaining M5.10 scenarios: the sandbox driver (A–G, J1, J2, J4), docs/59 §22.6 and §24.10.
